Chapter 01 · Fuel & Heritage

The Legacy ICE Era

For decades, Sri Lankan roads belonged to traditional petrol and diesel franchises backed by deep islandwide service workshops. Yet high fuel costs and 100%–120% ICE luxury taxes have compressed traditional Japanese share from 81% (2019) to 62% (2025).

Chapter 02 · Post-Ban Surge

The Rugged SUV & Jeep Wave

After a five-year import hiatus, Sri Lanka's market exploded nearly 5× to 360,117 registrations ($2.05B import bill). Bold new SUVs—like Jetour T2 (~27% of small SUVs) and JAECOO J5 HEV—stormed the LKR 15M–25M Mid-Range band with luxury styling at mainstream prices.

Chapter 03 · Blue Ocean Disruption

The Pure EV Takeover

S · Sense: Strategy Palette & Disruption
Diagnosing Sri Lanka's post-ban competitive terrain.

Environment: Adaptive / Shaping

Low predictability (tariff & FX shifts) paired with high malleability (60% EV luxury tax edge & rapid Chinese OEM entry). Classical 5-year plans age in months; winners vary, select, and scale fast.

The Innovator's Dilemma in Action

Incumbents defend high-margin ICE heritage while disruptors (BYD, Jetour, Jaecoo, Kaiyi) enter lower with superior cabin tech and climb straight into the mainstream.

C · Choose: ERRC Action Grid
Eliminate · Reduce · Raise · Create framework applied to Sri Lanka's EV transition.

Eliminate

Combustion engine complexity, high fuel dependency, and 100%–120% petrol/diesel luxury tax penalties.

Reduce

Landed price via vertical scale + 60% EV luxury tax tier; model refresh cycles cut to ~18 months vs. 48+ months.

Raise

ADAS driver-assist, large infotainment displays, and connected features delivered as standard in the LKR 10M–22M segment.

Create

Software-defined vehicles (OTA updates), bundled home-charging, and battery-health resale warranties.
