SCALE FRAMEWORK Strategic Diagnosis & Recommendations

Applying Angelo's SCALE Pathway directly to Sri Lanka's 2026 Automotive Market.

Strategy Palette Diagnosis: Adaptive / Shaping

Post-import-ban Sri Lanka is a low-predictability, high-malleability market driven by tax policy shifts, surging Chinese NEV supply, and rapid buyer migration. Classical multi-year plans fail here; winners vary, select, and scale fast.

The Innovator's Dilemma in Action

Legacy incumbents (United Motors, DIMO, Toyota, AMW) built moats on heritage & ICE service networks. Disruptors (BYD at 48.8% Oct 2026 share, Jetour at ~27% small SUV share, Jaecoo) entered at lower prices with higher standard tech — climbing straight into the mainstream.

Eliminate

Internal combustion engine complexity, high fuel dependency, and legacy 100%–120% petrol/diesel luxury tax penalties.

Reduce

Vehicle purchase price via vertical scale + 60% EV luxury tax tier; model refresh cycles cut to ~18 months vs. 48+ months for legacy makers.

Raise

In-car software, ADAS / driver-assist, and panoramic/premium cabins offered as standard in the Budget & Mid-Range LKR 10M–22M bands.

Create

Software-defined vehicles (OTA updates), ultra-low running cost urban mobility, and bundled home-charger + battery warranty ecosystems.
