# Phase 1: Master Player Registry, Sri Lanka Brand-New Automotive Market
_As of 25 Nov 2026. Brand-new vehicles only; used and reconditioned importers are excluded._

## 1. Market data sources

## 2. MECE rules applied
- Each brand appears under exactly one distributor.
- **GWM is split by powertrain:** ICE models (Haval Jolion, Poer) → **Senok** (Batch B); NEV range (Haval HEV/PHEV, ORA) → **David Pieris** (Batch A).
- **BMW is in transition:** Mels Automobiles (Melstacorp) appointed May 2026 → use **Mels** (Batch A) for BMW prices.
- **Group links:** Unimo (Perodua) sits under United Motors (Batch C).

## 3. VAT and vehicle taxes
- **VAT is 18%** (standard rate confirmed for 2026). Normalization rule: `Std_Price_Mn = Listed_Price × 1.18` if `Excl_VAT`.
- **Luxury tax advantage for EVs:** EVs face lower luxury tax (CIF > Rs 6.0M at 60%) compared to Petrol (Rs 5.0M at 100%) and Diesel (Rs 5.0M at 120%) — key strategic insight for the Blue Ocean Strategy Canvas.
