A 5× Market Rebound — And an EV Disruption Redrawing the Map

Following Sri Lanka's five-year vehicle import hiatus, registrations surged to 360,117 units ($2.05B import bill) in 2025. While legacy incumbents still control nationwide service networks, Japanese share fell from 81% to 62% as Chinese EVs—led by BYD (48.8% of Oct 2026 new cars)—leverage structural cost efficiency and a 60% vs. 100%+ EV luxury tax edge.

C · Choose: ERRC Action Grid (How New Entrants Win)
Eliminate · Reduce · Raise · Create framework applied to Sri Lanka's automotive transition.

Eliminate

Combustion engine complexity, high fuel dependency, and 100%–120% petrol/diesel luxury tax penalties.

Reduce

Landed price via vertical scale + 60% EV luxury tax tier; model refresh cycles compressed to ~18 months vs. 48+ months.

Raise

ADAS driver-assist, large infotainment displays, and connected features delivered as standard in the LKR 10M–22M segment.

Create

Software-defined vehicles (OTA updates), bundled home-charging, and battery-health resale warranties.
