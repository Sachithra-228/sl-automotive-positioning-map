SCALE STRATEGY Frameworks, Evidenced Offerings & 28-Player Registry

Strategy Palette: Adaptive / Shaping

Post-ban Sri Lanka is low-predictability, high-malleability. Tariff adjustments, EV tax advantages (60% luxury tax vs. 100–120% ICE), and aggressive Chinese entrants mean static Classical 5-year plans fail. Players must experiment, select, and scale fast.

The Innovator's Dilemma in Sri Lanka

Incumbents (Toyota Lanka, DIMO, United Motors, AMW) hold deep workshop moats, yet Japan's registration share slid from 81% (2019) to 62% (2025). Disruptors (BYD 48.8% Oct '26 new-car share; Jetour ~27% small SUVs) entered lower with higher tech and are taking the mainstream.

Eliminate

Combustion engine complexity, high petrol running costs, and 100%–120% ICE luxury tax penalties.

Reduce

Landed price via vertical scale + 60% EV luxury tax tier; model refresh cycles cut to ~18 months vs. 48+ months.

Raise

ADAS, large screens, and connected tech delivered as standard in the LKR 10M–22M Budget/Mid-Range tiers.

Create

Software-defined cars (OTA updates), bundled home-charging & battery health warranties (e.g., EVO Charge, ChargeNET 100+ sites).

~70%
